The digital menu for restaurants and bars streamlines the ordering process through a user-friendly application, providing guests with comprehensive information about meals.

A standout feature is the ability for guests to view the estimated preparation times and receive real-time updates. The ecosystem includes several apps: daShef digital menu, waiter, guest, and kitchen monitor.

This was my first project as a software engineer with a focus on the backend, and it was the moment I chose to continue in that direction. Although I worked alone, the support from experienced colleagues was invaluable.

This remains one of my favorite applications in my career.
